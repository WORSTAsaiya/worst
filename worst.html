How to get alerts in Discord

  1. Make a webhook. In your Discord server open Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook. Pick the channel, then press Copy Webhook URL.
  2. Paste it in the Webhook box on the right. You have 3 slots, so you can send different alerts to different channels.
  3. Ping (optional). Turn on Developer Mode in Discord (User Settings → Advanced), right-click a role or a person and press Copy ID. Paste it in the ping box and choose Role or User. Type Everyone to ping everyone.
  4. Choose the rarity. Open the Rarity dropdown and tick Secret, Eternal or Divine. Pick 1 or 2, or pick all 3 and it becomes All.
  5. Press Save. Your channel gets a "connected" message. Press Test to see a sample alert.

Alerts keep coming while this page is closed. Butterfly Bloom and Racing events are always sent. Remove deletes the selected webhook from our server. Have a key? Use Input key at the top for kg, mutation and join links.
